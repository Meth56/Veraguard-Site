How to lay the VeraGuard roll

The backing is self-adhesive, so there is no tape to buy, cut or carry. Three steps and the floor is covered.

The three steps

  1. 1

    Unroll along the floor

    The roll is 36 in wide and 100 ft long. Start at one edge of the area.

  2. 2

    Lay it adhesive side down

    Place the sheet where the crew will walk and work. No tape needed.

  3. 3

    Press it down

    Press the sheet into place, along the baseboards first, and carry on with the job.

Floors it is made for

Finished hardwood, engineered wood, parquet, bamboo, laminate, luxury vinyl plank, sheet vinyl, ceramic and porcelain tile, marble, natural stone, terrazzo, polished concrete, epoxy and similar floors, for temporary cover while work goes on. The top film is waterproof and the underside is anti-slip.
