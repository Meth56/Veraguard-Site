Who it is for

Crews that work on or around finished floors.

Painters

Cover finished floors while you work on walls and ceilings.

Renovators

Keep floors covered as trades move through the house.

Flooring installers

Protect finished rooms while you finish the next one.

Property managers

Cover floors during turnovers and in-building work.

How to use it

  1. Unroll

    Start at one edge of the area and roll the sheet out along the floor.

  2. Place

    Lay it where you need it. The self-adhesive side faces the floor.

  3. Press down

    Press the sheet into place, especially along baseboards.

Please test a small area first. Floors and finishes differ. Read the full steps.

Three layers, one sheet

Waterproof PE film on top, non-woven felt in the middle, and a self-adhesive, anti-slip layer underneath. One roll is 36 in x 100 ft and weighs about 12 lb.

Questions

Do I need tape?

No. The roll is self-adhesive, so it goes down without tape for normal installation.

Is it waterproof?

Yes. The top film is waterproof and helps protect against spills and moisture.

Which floors is it for?

Finished hardwood, tile, laminate, vinyl and similar floors, for temporary use while work is going on. Floors and finishes differ, so please test a small area first and tell us what you are protecting.

How long can I leave it down?

Ask us. Tell us your floor and how long the job runs, and we will answer for your case.

Can I take it up and use it again?

Ask us. We would rather answer for your floor than guess.

Can I get a sample?

Samples are not available. A small test patch from your first roll is the way to check.

Do you deliver?

Yes, to your job site when you order 3 or more rolls, within 30 km in the GTA. Further out? Ask us.
