Laid in three steps. Test a small area first.

1

Roll the sheet out

Start at one edge of the room and unroll along the floor.

2

Lay the roll adhesive side down

Place it where the crew will walk and work. The sticky side faces the floor.

3

Press the sheet flat

Press it down by hand, along the baseboards first.

Who lays it

Painters, renovators, flooring installers and property managers lay VeraGuard over finished floors, so the ladders, boots and paint trays of every trade land on the sheet.

Three layers in every roll

  • PE film on top

    Waterproof, so a spill stays on the sheet.

  • Non-woven felt

    The soft body between the film and your floor.

  • Adhesive backing

    Anti-slip, and holds the sheet in place without tape.

Does the roll need tape?

No. The backing is self-adhesive, so the sheet goes down without tape.

How many rolls does a job need?

Divide the floor area by 300 and round up: a 2,500 sq ft job takes 9 rolls.

How long can the floor protection stay down?

It depends on the floor and the job. Tell us both and we answer for your case, reuse included.

Is there a sample roll?

No samples. Test a small patch from your first roll on the floor you are protecting.
