Privacy and cookies

Plain version: analytics is off until you say yes, and we do not store your quote request on this site.

Analytics

With your permission, we use Google Analytics to count visits, see which pages people read, and see which links bring them here. This sets cookies in your browser. If you decline, or ignore the notice, analytics does not load and no analytics cookies are set.

The quote request form

The form does not send anything to a server. It opens your email app with your details filled in, and the message reaches us only when you press send. We use what you send to reply to your request.

Links in our emails

Links in our emails may carry tags (for example, utm_source=email) so we can tell which message brought a visit. The tags contain no personal information.
